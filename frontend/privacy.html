Privacy notice

Data minimisation

ShareBite uses email and display name for authentication, general collection areas, food listing details, photographs and private messages. It does not require a public phone number or a publicly displayed residential address.

Password security

Passwords are handled by Amazon Cognito and are not stored in the ShareBite DynamoDB table or frontend code.

Retention and rights

Expired listings, messages, account data and backups require documented retention periods. Users should be able to request access, correction and deletion.

Prototype status

This is a university prototype and should not be described as fully UK GDPR compliant without a complete legal and operational assessment.
